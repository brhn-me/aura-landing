# Demo media credits

The demo library on this site (the app demo, the screenshots, the page's small demos) uses only public-domain
works and works under Creative Commons licences that allow reuse (CC0, or CC BY with credit). Changes made:
songs and videos cut to short clips and re-encoded; the songs' covers are frames of their own videos (or the photos listed) with the title added; the lyrics shown are the ones the artist published with the song; some tags are made messy on purpose to show
Review.
