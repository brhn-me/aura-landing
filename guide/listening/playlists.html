Playlists and smart playlists

Playlists

  1. On a song's ⋯, choose Playlist ▸ and a playlist, or a new one. Or press + beside Playlists in the sidebar.
  2. Open the playlist and drag rows into your order (in Custom order).

The queue's ⋯ also has Save as playlist.

Smart playlists New in 1.5

  1. Choose New smart playlist beside Playlists in the sidebar (on a phone, in More).
  2. Under Rules, pick a Field (Title, Artist, Album, Composer, Genre, Year, My rating, My plays, Last played, Liked …), a Condition and a Value. Add a rule for more.
  3. Choose Songs that match all of these rules or any of these rules, an Order, and At most how many songs.
  4. Save. The playlist follows your library and your plays by itself; Edit rules changes it later.

Note Smart playlists come from your server: they aren’t in phone mode or offline yet.
