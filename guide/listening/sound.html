Sound and quality

  • Settings → General → Streaming Quality: Automatic (best) plays the original file and steps down only on a slow connection; Original, High, Normal and Low are fixed. Data Saver uses Low on mobile data.
  • Settings → Playback: Crossfade, Gapless, the Song Cache (songs kept on this device so they play through a network drop) and Read Ahead.
  • Settings → Audio: a 10-band Equalizer with presets, and Volume normalization (ReplayGain).
  • Speed: 0.5× to 2× without changing the pitch, from ⋯ → Speed in Now Playing or the video's 1× button.
