1.5.0-alpha.27

2026-11-13Pre-release

A faster start: the app loads only what its layout needs before the first screen. Also an off switch for More like this, the song vectors working in the Docker image, fewer false duplicates, and Aura's website, demo and guide in the repo.

Show all changes

Added

  • Administration → Features → "More like this & search by meaning": off by default; while off, no song is read by the AI model for it and the menu item and search chip are hidden (B-301).
  • The project's MIT licence.
  • Aura's website in the repo (landing/): the landing page, a live demo of the app with a small library of free music and films, a guide, download, changelog, credits and licence pages, and a script to publish it (not published yet).

Changed

  • Start-up is lighter: 294 KB of JavaScript instead of 325 KB before the first screen; the web layout and the phone app's layout each load only their own parts; the total is 559 KB instead of 570 (B-308).
  • Sample device names in the app's built-in examples are neutral.

Fixed

  • Song vectors are on in the Docker image: the vector extension loads from the image itself (the container's /tmp can't run code) (B-301).
  • Review: songs whose title is only a number or a placeholder ("01", "Track 05"), or with no artist from different albums, are no longer offered as duplicates (B-373).

Internal

  • JavaScript budgets: start-up 296 KB, total 560 KB.

1.5.0-alpha.26

2026-11-13Pre-release

More like this and search by meaning: Aura learns how your songs feel from their lyrics, tags and credits, with your own local AI model, and finds the ones that belong together.

Show all changes

Added

  • "More like this" on a song (its ⋯ menu and Now Playing's): songs close to it by their lyrics, tags and credits, mixed with your likes, plays, artist and genre; it works before the index is ready too (B-301).
  • Search "By meaning": find songs by what they are about ("songs about rain"), beside the word search; each result says why it matched (B-301).
  • Song vectors on your server: the local AI model reads each song's tags, credits and lyrics (as Aura shows them, with Review's changes) and stores a vector in the library database (sqlite-vec); paced like the other AI work, re-reading only songs that change. Administration → Server → Embedding model (default bge-m3, ollama pull bge-m3 on the AI server) (B-301).

Changed

  • The lyrics visualizer styles are called Lyrics and Lyrics Cloud, listed before Off (owner).
  • Autoplay, the radio from a song and the queue's ideas also use the songs' closeness while the index is ready (B-301).

Fixed

  • A menu taller than the screen (a phone held sideways) fits and scrolls; its submenus stay usable.
  • "Not an artist" (or an edit) could leave an empty artist behind when its songs were changed in a certain order.

Internal

  • Migration 0066 (song vectors); sqlite-vec 0.1.9 bundled for every platform.
  • The JavaScript budgets are 570 KB total and 326 KB at start-up for this alpha only (owner); alpha.27 lowers them.

1.5.0-alpha.24

2026-11-12Pre-release

Review, round two: fewer and better cards. The same fix across the library is one card, a song's fixes are one card, safe kinds can apply by themselves, the most played songs come first, and you choose how long a round is. Review learns spam phrases, artist spellings, languages and genres from your decisions, and many more junk tags are cleaned. The Review page gives the cards all its space.

Show all changes

Added

  • Group cards: the same fix on many songs anywhere in the library is one card listing each song before → after, with a tick to leave a song out; a song's cards on different fields are one card with a tick per field. The Kind filters count songs and cards (B-355).
  • Safe kinds can apply by themselves: in What Review learned each kind is "Ask me" or "Apply by itself" (suggested when you accept it as is 95 % of the time); applied fixes are listed under "Applied by itself" with Undo per fix and per group. Fixes from the web, MusicBrainz or the AI that rewrite a title, artist or album, merges, splits and duplicates always ask (B-354).
  • Round length: Quick (5), Normal (20), Long (50) or Keep going; a round ends with what changed ("12 songs fixed, library 73 % clean"). A quick mode for easy cards (one field, sure): → or a swipe right accepts, ← or left discards. Now Playing shows "Fix this song" when the playing song has a card (B-357).
  • Library health: the share of songs with no open card, on Review's title row and in its status (B-356).
  • The metadata editor shows the song's open Review suggestions under their fields (Use, ×, Use all) and Review's name pills; saving decides those cards (B-359).
  • Review learns spam phrases: a phrase taken out of three or more songs goes on the library's list (Administration → Server) and becomes one card for every song still holding it (B-344).
  • "Wrong lyrics" on lyrics cards: that text is never offered for the song again, nor for songs with other titles once wrong for two; the song's own wrong lyrics can be cleared the same way (B-364).
  • Start Review over has its own confirmation with "Download a backup" first; Administration → Server restores a backup, the changes and their Undo with it (B-349).
  • A Review icon in the header beside notifications, with the count of open cards, lit briefly when new cards arrive (B-350).

Changed

  • Review learns from your decisions: a language you set is proposed for the folder's other songs, a genre you remove from a folder's songs is removed from the rest, your artist spellings are used from then on, the AI proposes a genre only with evidence, "(Remix)" moves from the title to the version, merged artist names are cut to the main one, "(2)" copy marks and "Title-Band" endings go (B-347).
  • The cards come in a better order: the songs you play most first, then big albums and artists, kinds mixed (B-356).
  • The Review page shows only its title row and the cards: its status and the AI warning are behind a status button; the "accepted N of M as they were" lines are gone; a small ring beside Accept / Discard replaces the progress bar; cards show the song's folder (B-341, B-351, B-352, B-353).
  • Now Playing's Review dialog looks like Review's cards; "Suggestion 1 of 2 for this song" (B-361).
  • Review's Show dialog shows every Kind filter (B-349).

Fixed

  • An accepted or edited card that cleared the artist left it in place ("02.Chal"); the songs it happened to are repaired at the upgrade (B-345).
  • Titles come from the file name when the tags hold a cut-off name, an uploader's credit or a shop's advert ("02.Chal Chale", "Javeda Zindagi (Tose Naina Lagey)"); no suggestion keeps a leading track number or an unclosed bracket (B-338, B-345).
  • "Track 02" songs and placeholder albums get their names from the file and the folder (B-339).
  • Junk around a real name is cleaned ("Jodhaa Akbar - www.123musiq.com - ® Riya collections ®" → "Jodhaa Akbar"), also in genres, lyrics and comments (B-340).
  • A year in an album, folder or file name ("Mohabbatein-2000") becomes a year suggestion and leaves the album's name (B-343).
  • More junk cleaned: sites and credits in composer, lyricist and album artist, shop and uploader stamps, placeholder genres and albums, ID3's stray "Blues", comment noise; "Music: X | Lyrics: Y" split (B-346).
  • Lyrics found for songs with different titles, or in a script that doesn't fit the song's language, are no match; audiobook releases never match a song (B-347).

Internal

  • Migrations 0059–0063 (the clear repair, spam phrases, wrong lyrics, group keys, applied fixes).
  • The JavaScript total budget is raised from 546 to 561 KB for this alpha (owner); B-308 wins it back.

Also smart playlists, song versions (covers, remixes, live …), several servers in phone mode, and songs changed by Review updating at once in Now Playing and the queue.

A version only guessed from a title's note ("(Live at …)") is a Review card, one per album when the whole album is live; a matched MusicBrainz recording proposes one too; the metadata editor has a Version field. The first scan after the upgrade re-reads every song's tags once (B-315).
  • Phone mode remembers up to five servers: Settings → Library lists each (who, last used), one tap back with its own sign-in; Change server keeps you signed in to the one you leave, Sign out and Forget sign you out of it (B-302).
  • AI cards show the file's text the answer came from (B-293), and whether a MusicBrainz match confirms or disputes it; confirmed cards come first (B-294).
  • One Review card for the same fix across an album's or a folder's songs ("Artist X for the 12 songs of Album"), listing the songs; undo puts each song back (B-295).
  • On a touch screen a Review card swiped right is accepted, left discarded, up skipped, with Undo (B-297).

Changed

  • What Review learned counts every decision, also of cards since gone, and shows how often each kind is accepted as is (B-292).
  • The AI model gets a few of this library's own corrections as examples with each request; nothing leaves the server (B-296).
  • Review shows its background pass: songs left, the next round, how long a round took against its budget, and when it stopped at its limit (B-298).
  • Review uses the full width; on computers ← Library goes back (B-335).
  • Duplicates are Review cards, one group per card, while the duplicates switch is on; the Duplicates page is gone (its address opens Review) (B-336).

Fixed

  • A song changed by Review, the metadata editor or a lyrics save shows its new title, artist, album, artwork and lyrics at once in Now Playing, the queue and the phone's notification, without stopping playback (B-337).
  • Play next from a remote could land after a song added at the same moment (B-327).

Internal

  • Small modules shared by four or more on-demand screens are one file, so smart playlists fit the JavaScript budget without raising it (545.4 of 546 KB).

1.5.0-alpha.18

2026-11-08Pre-release

The alpha.17 review batch: the owner's feedback from the phone and the web (fb#001–fb#019) and the review's own points (B-265–B-290), plus review round 3's High fixes: search over every field and the lyrics, Request review and Look for lyrics in the metadata editor, a lyrics sync tool, the Android app's mini video player and landscape Now Playing, tidier Devices and Sources pages, phone mode keeping the server, and thicker playing bars.

Show all changes

Security

  • A phone's sync never stalls on an odd value: a far-off clock or a broken length is pulled into range instead of refused; every other route refuses out-of-range input (B-164).
  • A secret scan (bun run check:secrets) checks the tracked files, the built web app and, on request, the whole git history for keys and tokens; it runs with every test run and in the release gate. The history and the web app hold none (B-159, B-160).
  • Each user's own data only, checked route by route: every route that names a playlist, play, station, device, upload or session of another user answers 404 as if it didn't exist (admins too), and lists hold only the caller's own; an audit of every table with a user column and every route found no gap, and a new test fails on any route or table added without a decision (B-163).
  • Every input the server takes has limits: each path parameter, query and body field has a maximum length, range or item count (ids at most 64 characters, cursors 512, timestamps up to 2100, positions 7 days, sizes 1 PiB), and a request over one is refused with the usual validation error before it reaches the code; a test walks every route and the WebSocket's messages and fails on an open end (B-164).

Added

  • Search finds songs and videos by their composer, lyricist, cast, original artist, language, year, genres and file name (search, the header's instant results and the list filters), and the search page by their lyrics too, after every other match (a title always beats a lyric line); a song found that way says why under its artist ("In the lyrics: “…”", "Composer: …"). Migration 0049 adds the fields to the search index and rebuilds it (B-265).
  • The metadata editor has Request review (Review looks at this one song or video again, even if it was asked or decided before; an accepted change is never undone) and Look for lyrics (LRCLIB, its card in Review) (B-266).
  • Lyrics: an admin can sync timed lyrics in the lyrics panel: tap the line being sung, or nudge ±0.5 s, then Save (kept as a metadata edit of the song) (B-285).
  • The Android app: a mini video player (the video keeps playing in a small box when you leave Now Playing, no switch to audio) (B-279); a full-screen button on the portrait video, turning landscape or staying upright by the video's shape (B-272); the video's controls centred over the picture on touch screens (B-273).
  • The Android app: switching to This phone keeps the server's address and the sign-in; Settings → Library shows "Back to <server> (as <you>)", one tap (B-289).
  • Stats' top lists show the artist's photo and each song's and video's picture (B-282).

Changed

  • Devices: one tidy list: each device its icon, name, one line ("This device · Online · playing", "Last seen …") and one ⋮ (Rename, Forget); the active ones first, older ones collapsed under their count with "Remove all older".
